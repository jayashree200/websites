About SSA Solutions

A decade of dependable cleaning across Bangalore

Built on trust, professionalism, and a genuine commitment to clean spaces.

SSA Solutions was founded in 2014 with a simple belief: every space deserves to be clean, hygienic, and welcoming. Over the past decade, we've grown from a small housekeeping team to a trusted partner for hundreds of homes, retail chains, and corporate offices across Bangalore.

We train every staff member in modern cleaning techniques, use eco-friendly, hospital-grade chemicals, and deploy professional equipment to deliver results that speak for themselves.
